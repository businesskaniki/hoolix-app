What We Offer Abroad

EagleCore supports foreign nationals with Kenya work permits, passes, travel authorization, foreign national registration, citizenship services and permanent residence applications.

  • Permits

    A Work Permit is issued by the Director of Immigration Services under section 40 of the Kenya Citizenship and Immigration Act, 2011. It allows foreign nationals to enter Kenya and engage in trade, farming, business, professional employment, missionary activities or residence.

    Class A

    Prospecting and mining activities.

    Class B

    Agriculture and animal husbandry.

    Class C

    Prescribed profession practice.

    Class D

    Specific employment by an employer, government or approved aid scheme.

    Class F

    Specific manufacturing in Kenya.

    Class G

    Specific trade, business, consultancy or non-prescribed profession.

    Class I

    Approved religious activities for non-EAC nationals.

    Class K

    Ordinary residents with assured independent income.

    Class M

    Refugees and their spouses seeking employment or occupation.

    Class N

    Digital nomads working remotely for an overseas employer.

    Class P

    UN, diplomatic, inter-governmental or international NGO staff.

    Class Q

    Professionals working for religious or charitable organizations.

    Class R

    East African Community nationals residing or working in Kenya.

    Exemption

    Persons exempted from work permit requirements by cabinet secretary notice.

  • Passes

    Kenya issues special, dependant, student/research/internship passes under Section 26 of the Kenya Citizenship and Immigration Regulations, 2012.

    Special Pass

    Temporary business, trade or profession visits. Fee: $200/month for non-EAC nationals; free for EAC members.

    Dependant Pass

    For spouses, parents or guardians of eligible Kenyan citizens and permit holders. Fees: Ksh 20,000 for Kenyan dependants; Ksh 10,000 for work permit or permanent resident dependants; free for EAC nationals.

    Student Pass

    For foreign students admitted to approved Kenyan institutions. Fee: $100/year for non-EAC nationals; free for EAC members.

    Internship/Research Pass

    For internships or academic research in Kenya. Fee: Ksh 15,000/year for non-EAC nationals; free for EAC members.

  • Foreign Nationals Management

    We help foreign nationals comply with Kenya Citizenship and Immigration Act 2011 requirements for registration, alien certificates, re-entry passes, visitor pass extensions and permit endorsements.

    Alien Card

    Registration for foreigners residing in Kenya longer than 90 days.

    Re-entry Pass

    For dependent pass holders returning to Kenya.

    Visitor Pass Extension

    Extend stay up to 6 months. Fee: $50.

    Permit Endorsement

    Passport stamping and transfer of residence details.

  • Citizenship Services

    EagleCore assists with citizenship applications under the Kenya Citizenship and Immigration Act, 2011 and the Constitution of Kenya, 2010.

    Regaining Citizenship (Form 1)

    For Kenyan citizens by birth who lost citizenship. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 50,000 issuance.

    Dual Citizenship Declaration (Form 3)

    Required for Kenyans with another nationality. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 10,000 issuance.

    Endorsement on Non-Kenyan Passport (Form 4)

    Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 750,000 issuance.

    Renunciation (Form 6)

    Voluntary renunciation of Kenyan citizenship. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 50,000 issuance.

    Citizenship by Marriage (Form 8)

    For foreigners married to Kenyans for 7+ years. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 50,000 for EAC, Ksh 100,000 for non-EAC.

    Widows and Widowers (Form 9)

    Spouses of deceased Kenyans married for 7+ years. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 20,000 for EAC, Ksh 50,000 for non-EAC.

    Lawful Residents (Form 10)

    Long-term residents of 7+ years. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 1,000,000 issuance.

    Children and Dependants (Form 11)

    For children born before parent acquired citizenship or dependants of citizen parents. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 100,000 issuance.

    Stateless Persons (Form 13)

    Persons without enforceable citizenship claim living in Kenya since 1963. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 10,000 issuance.

    Migrants (Form 14)

    Persons who migrated before December 12, 1963. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 10,000 issuance.

    Descendants of Stateless Persons and Migrants (Form 15)

    Adults aged 18+ whose parents were eligible stateless persons or migrants. Fees: Ksh 20,000 application, Ksh 10,000 issuance.

  • Permanent Residence

    Permanent residence in Kenya is governed by sections 37, 38 and 39 of the Kenya Citizenship and Immigration Act, 2011.

    Ex-Kenyan (Category A)

    Former Kenyan citizens renouncing citizenship whose domicile country does not allow dual citizenship. Fee: Ksh 50,000 processing, Ksh 100,000 issuance.

    Lawful Residents (Category B)

    Holders of work permits for 7+ years and continuous Kenyan residence for 3 years. Fee: Ksh 50,000 processing, Ksh 750,000 requisite.

    Children of Kenyan Citizens (Category C)

    Children born to Kenyan parents but holding another nationality. Fee: Ksh 20,000 processing, Ksh 750,000 requisite.

    Spouse of Kenyan Citizen (Category D)

    Married to a Kenyan citizen for 3+ years in a bona fide marriage. Fee: Ksh 50,000 processing, Ksh 150,000 requisite.
